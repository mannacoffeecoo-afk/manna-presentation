How it works

Working with MANNA is simple.

  1. Tell us about your event

    Share the date, location, estimated guest count and the experience you're interested in.

  2. Receive your custom proposal

    We'll build a service recommendation around your event.

  3. Reserve your date

    Once you're ready to move forward, we'll guide you through reserving your event.

  4. Finalize the details

    We'll confirm the menu, guest count and event logistics with you before the event.

  5. Enjoy the experience

    Our team arrives, sets up, serves your guests and handles breakdown.
